Applying for the CAF housing allowance as a student

What the CAF allowance is

The CAF (Caisse d'Allocations Familiales) administers the APL and ALS housing allowances — a monthly contribution toward your rent, available to most students in France regardless of nationality, including non-EU students on a valid long-stay visa. It doesn't cover your full rent, but it's meaningful enough to budget around, typically covering a meaningful fraction of a student's monthly rent depending on your city and housing type.

1. Timing: apply as soon as you have a lease

You can apply from the day your lease starts — don't wait for your OFII appointment or student ID to be finalized. The allowance is calculated from your application date, not backdated significantly, so a delayed application is money you don't get back.

2. Documents to have ready

  • Your signed lease (bail) or landlord attestation
  • RIB (bank account details) for a French bank account
  • Passport and valid long-stay visa (VLS-TS) or residence permit
  • Proof of enrollment (certificat de scolarité) from your institution
  • Your OFII validation, once completed

3. Create your account and declare your resources

The CAF online application asks for your estimated resources for the relevant period. As a new arrival with no French income history, you'll typically declare your available funds/scholarship rather than a salary — be accurate rather than conservative, since incorrect declarations can trigger repayment demands later.

4. What slows applications down

The most common delays are a lease that doesn't clearly show your name and address, a RIB submitted before the bank account is fully active, and OFII validation not yet completed. CAF processing itself typically takes a few weeks once your file is complete — submitting an incomplete file to "start the clock" earlier usually backfires, since the file is paused until missing documents arrive.
