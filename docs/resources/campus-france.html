Campus France: the complete application walkthrough

What Campus France actually is

Campus France India runs the "Études en France" (CEF) procedure — a mandatory step for most Indian students applying to French higher education institutions. It isn't an admissions body itself: it collects and forwards your academic file to the institutions you apply to, and later to the consulate for your visa. Confusing the two is the single most common source of delay.

1. Create your Études en France account

Registration opens on the official Études en France portal. You'll need scanned copies of your academic transcripts (10th, 12th, and any completed degree), a passport-size photo, and your passport's bio page. Keep file sizes and formats exactly to the portal's specification — rejected uploads are a frequent, avoidable delay.

2. Build your academic file

This is where most applications lose strength. Beyond transcripts, you'll write a study project (projet d'études) explaining why this specific program, in this specific school, fits your background and plans. Generic statements ("France has good universities") are immediately recognizable to reviewers who read hundreds of these. A stronger structure:

  • What you've done so far, and the specific gap it leaves
  • Why this program closes that gap better than an equivalent one at home
  • What you intend to do with the qualification afterward

3. Submit to your chosen institutions

Most institutions review CEF files on rolling or seasonal deadlines — check each program's page directly rather than assuming a single national deadline. Apply to more than one program where your budget allows: a single-institution strategy has no fallback if that specific program is competitive that year.

4. Prepare for the interview

Many CEF applicants are called for a short interview, usually 15–20 minutes, conducted in English or French depending on the program. Expect to be asked to justify your program choice, explain your study project in your own words (not recite it), and describe your plans after graduation. Reviewers are checking for coherence between what you wrote and what you say — not for a rehearsed script.

The Campus France Kit includes the study project framework, a document checklist, and an interview prep list used in this guide.
