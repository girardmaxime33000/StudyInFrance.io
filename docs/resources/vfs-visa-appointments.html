Visa

Booking your VFS visa appointment without losing weeks

What VFS Global actually does

VFS Global is the outsourced visa application centre that collects your file and biometrics on behalf of the French consulate — it does not decide your visa. Its only role in the process is scheduling your appointment and forwarding a complete, correctly formatted file. Most delays at this stage come from booking mistakes, not from the visa decision itself.

1. Complete France-Visas before you touch VFS

You cannot book a VFS appointment without a completed application on the France-Visas portal first. Fill it out carefully — the appointment booking system pulls your reference number directly from it, and a mismatched name or passport number between the two systems is a common cause of appointment cancellations.

2. Watch the slot release pattern, not just availability

VFS slots are typically released in batches rather than continuously. Applicants who check once and give up on "no slots available" often miss releases that happen at specific times. Check consistently across a few days rather than assuming the calendar is genuinely empty.

3. Prepare your document set before booking, not after

Once you have a confirmed slot, you generally cannot reschedule easily without losing your place in the queue. Have your full document set ready before you book:

  • Printed France-Visas application form and confirmation
  • Valid passport with at least the required validity beyond your stay
  • Proof of admission (attestation from your institution)
  • Proof of financial resources for the length of your stay
  • Accommodation proof for your arrival period
  • Student health insurance certificate

4. At the appointment itself

The VFS appointment is administrative: document submission and biometrics (photo and fingerprints). It's not an interview about your study project — that was handled earlier at Campus France. Arrive early, bring both originals and photocopies of every document, and expect the appointment itself to take under 30 minutes if your file is complete.

The SOP/CV Pack and a coaching review can help you tighten your paperwork before you book.
